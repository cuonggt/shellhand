**A host key you were being asked to approve could go unseen.** When the
question did not fit the window — large text, or a narrow one — the terminal
wrapped it, and the app looked for it a line at a time and found it in neither
half. So nothing was asked: ssh sat on the other end waiting for an answer
that was never coming, and the session showed as connected throughout. A
wrapped question is read now, and what comes up is the fingerprint and the
warning above it rather than the whole screen around them.

**A session said it was connected before anything had answered.** The test for
"has the far side said anything" counted a terminal that had heard nothing as
output, because an untouched buffer is its own blank rows rather than nothing
at all. Every session therefore reported connected about a fifth of a second
after it opened, whether or not the host was there or willing. It says
connecting until something actually arrives.

**A download that cannot be written no longer takes the app with it.** A full
disk, a volume pulled out mid-transfer, permissions changed underneath — the
write that noticed raised something no Swift `catch` can see, so the app
vanished where it meant to say the file could not be written.

Nothing else is visible from the outside. The suite went from 383 checks to
403, and the terminal is in it for the first time: the watchdog, what it makes
of what is on screen, and the reading of the screen itself, all without a pty
— the terminal is handed its output directly instead. Two of the three fixes
above were found that way, by tests written for coverage rather than after a
bug report.
